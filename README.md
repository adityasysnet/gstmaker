## 🚀 About GST Maker

**GST Maker** is an all-in-one GST billing, invoicing, accounting assistance, and business management platform designed to simplify financial operations for Indian businesses.

Managing invoices, calculating GST, tracking expenses, monitoring payments, maintaining inventory, and preparing tax reports shouldn't require multiple complicated applications.

GST Maker brings these essential business operations together in one modern platform.

With intelligent automation, AI-powered assistance, professional PDF invoice templates, automated tax calculations, customer management, inventory tracking, and reporting tools, GST Maker helps businesses manage their daily financial workflows more efficiently.

Whether you're an independent freelancer, consultant, service provider, startup, or growing business, GST Maker provides the tools to manage your billing and business operations in one place.

**Our vision:** Make professional GST billing and business management accessible, simple, and efficient for every Indian entrepreneur.

## ✨ What Makes GST Maker Different?

- **AI-Powered Automation:** Generate invoice line items, identify HSN/SAC codes, process expenses, and get business insights with AI.
- **Complete Billing Solution:** Manage tax invoices, quotations, proforma invoices, recurring invoices, and purchase bills.
- **GST-Focused Workflows:** Automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST calculations, ITC tracking, and GSTR reporting assistance.
- **Professional Invoice Designs:** Seven professionally designed PDF invoice themes.
- **Business Management:** Manage customers, suppliers, products, inventory, expenses, and staff.
- **CA Collaboration:** Share business reports and tax information with accountants through a dedicated CA portal.
- **AI Integrations:** Connect your GST Maker workspace with supported AI tools through MCP.
- **Free GST Utilities:** Access useful GST calculators and verification tools without creating an account.
- **Mobile-Friendly Experience:** Use GST Maker across desktop and mobile devices with Progressive Web App (PWA) support.

## 🧾 Complete Feature Overview

### 1. GST Invoicing & Billing

Create professional GST-compliant invoices for your business.

- B2B and B2C tax invoices.
- Automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST calculations.
- Place of Supply-based tax calculation.
- HSN/SAC code support.
- Flat and percentage-based discounts.
- Custom invoice numbering.
- Business logo and digital signature support.
- Professional invoice PDF generation.
- Multiple invoice copy formats:
  - Original for Recipient.
  - Duplicate for Transporter.
  - Triplicate for Supplier.
- Direct invoice sharing through WhatsApp.
- Secure public invoice viewing and downloading links.
- Invoice management and payment status tracking.

### 2. Quotations & Estimates

Prepare professional quotations and estimates for customers.

- Create quotations for products and services.
- Generate downloadable quotation PDFs.
- Share quotations with customers.
- Track quotation expiry dates.
- Convert approved quotations into tax invoices with one click.
- Create free quotations without logging in.

### 3. Proforma Invoices

Create advance payment requests before delivering products or services.

- Generate professional proforma invoices.
- Download and share proforma PDFs.
- Request advance payments.
- Track proforma billing.
- Convert proforma invoices into final tax invoices.
- Avoid duplicate data entry.
- Create free proforma invoices without logging in.
